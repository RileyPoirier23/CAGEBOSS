## One line

A satirical, R-rated pixel-art MMA game: fight your way up from a soup-kitchen gym, or run the whole promotion and watch it all go wrong.

## Short description (about 50 words)

CAGE BOSS is a free pixel-art MMA game that roasts the whole sport. Throw every punch yourself in Road To Champion, a five-chapter story that starts in a soup-kitchen gym in the Maritimes. Or be the president: work the paperwork desk, build the cards, survive the press conference and watch it all go wrong on pay-per-view.

## Long description

Uncle Ray's soup-kitchen gym is $8,000 behind on rent. A trust-fund kid with a cold plunge owns the local belt, a leprechaun owns the regional one, and at the end of five chapters a six-foot-seven undefeated champion is waiting with a referee he pays. Ray has exactly one fighter. You.

**Road To Champion** is a hands-on fighting game: directional strikes, feints and footwork, a clinch game (Thai plum, underhooks, the fence), takedowns, sixteen submissions, and knockdowns you can jump on for the finish. You can watch it side-on, top-down or on the broadcast camera. Between fights you train, cut weight, sign contracts (read them first), do press conferences and face-offs, and climb four leagues to a superfight.

**Legacy Mode** unlocks after the story: the same career with no script, your own gym, and a promotion of your own when you retire.

**Promoter Mode** puts you behind the president's desk. Inspect the paperwork like *Papers, Please*: forged medicals, fake licences, weigh-in sheets and the fighter who "only had water." Then build the cards, keep the owner happy, handle the scandals and watch fight night unfold with a ring announcer, a commentary booth and a morning paper.

**Quick Fight** puts any two fighters in the cage, against the CPU or a friend on the same screen.

## Features

- Three ways to play: Road To Champion (story), Legacy Mode, and Promoter Mode
- Real-time fights: strikes, clinch, takedowns, ground and pound, 16 submissions, knockdowns, fouls, doctor stoppages
- Five story chapters with pixel cutscenes, a boss in every league, and a superfight
- 370+ fighters and parodies, rankings that move, title belts
- A Papers, Please-style desk with documents, stamps, a rulebook and citations
- Ring announcer intros, a commentary booth, tale of the tape, press conferences, and a morning paper
- A cutman mini-game between rounds
- A 17-track soundtrack from independent artists
- Couch versus, controller support, touch controls
