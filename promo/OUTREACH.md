## Tips

- **Size:** small and mid creators (5k to 200k followers) answer far more often than the big ones, and their audiences trust them more. Start there.
- **Format:** send the short DM to TikTok and Instagram creators, and the email to YouTubers and podcasts (their business email is usually under **About** on YouTube).
- **Pace:** about 10 personal messages a day beats 100 copied ones.
- **Tracking:** keep a simple list with name, platform, date sent, follow-up date and reply. Follow up once only.
- **What to attach:** the vertical clip that matches what they post (fight clip for MMA pages, story clip for gaming and story channels, promoter clip for strategy and sim channels).
- **Be honest that it's free.** That's the hook: nobody's being sold anything.
