# Console ads (plan)

Three ads, made **once the console builds run** so they show real console footage:
one for Xbox, one for PlayStation, and one with gameplay from every platform.

The PC trailer pipeline already exists: the game is driven by a script, captured, and cut
with music from the soundtrack. Each console ad is the same pipeline with the game in that
console's mode (`?platform=xbox` or `?platform=playstation`), so every on-screen button is
the right one. For the real thing we capture from dev kits.

## Rules for using the console names

- Words like "Xbox Series X|S" and "PlayStation 5" in the ad are fine to state the
  platform. **Official logos, badges and the end slates** come from each program's brand
  guidelines (they're in the partner portals) and must be used exactly as given.
- No console hardware in the footage unless their guidelines allow it.
- Rating: the ESRB/PEGI badge from the IARC rating goes on the end slate.

## 2. PlayStation ad (30 s)

The same structure with a different opening and different highlights, so the two don't look
like copies: open on the promoter desk (a forged medical getting stamped DENIED), PlayStation
button names in the fight, and close on two-player versus on one couch. End slate per
PlayStation's guidelines.

## 3. "Everywhere" ad (45 s)

A split-screen montage: the same fight on PC, Xbox and PlayStation (and phone, once it's
out), cut on the beat. Each panel is labelled with its platform. It ends on the full
platform list and 506clicks.ca.

## Deliverables per ad

- 1920×1080 MP4 (H.264), plus a 1080×1920 vertical cut for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
- A 15-second cut-down.
- A thumbnail and three stills for the store pages.
